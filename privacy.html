Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Who uses the app

Giggliwink Uploader is used only by the Giggliwink team to post our own videos to our own accounts. It has no other users.

What data it uses

  • A sign-in token from YouTube and TikTok for our own Giggliwink accounts, so the tool can upload our videos.
  • Our own account's basic profile info (name and avatar), only to confirm the right account is connected.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or sell any data about viewers or other users.
  • We do not use cookies, ads or tracking on this website.
  • We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Storage and deletion

Tokens are stored only on our own computer and can be removed at any time by disconnecting the app in the YouTube or TikTok account settings.

Contact

Questions: contact us through our YouTube channel.
